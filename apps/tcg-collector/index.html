Overview

Your card collection app allows users to browse and manage cards from various Trading Card Games (TCGs) such as Pokémon, Magic, One Piece, and Dragon Ball. Users can currently view cards in English, and while filtering options are not yet available, they are planned for future updates. You're also working on a side menu for easier navigation, although it has limited main options for now. The app is optimized for performance, ensuring efficient card storage and retrieval.
